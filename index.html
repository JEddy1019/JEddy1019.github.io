What Timmy does

Support for the day you actually have.

01 / Capture

Less to keep in your head.

Record tasks and estimates through a private conversation. Requested corrections update the original task and are checked against the saved result.

02 / Reconcile

A clear place to restart.

Review today's recorded task state and unfinished work from the previous week. Partial work normally continues on the same task.

03 / Choose

Match the next step to your energy.

Compare available time and current energy with saved tasks. Recommendations are suggestions; they do not silently reschedule your work.

About this site

Information, without a public dashboard.

This is the information site for an owner-operated personal assistant. It offers no public registration, payment flow or Google sign-in. The assistant is used through the owner's existing private messaging setup.

For data handling and access removal, read the Privacy policy. For the scope of this personal-use service, read the Terms of use. The support contact is the contact shown in the assistant's Google consent configuration.

Terms of use

Effective November 12, 2026

Timmy is an owner-operated personal time-management assistant. This site explains the assistant; it does not offer a public account or subscription service.

Access and intended use

Use Timmy only through the operator-authorized private setup and with accounts or data you are permitted to access. Google access requires consent from the account holder. This site does not grant access to the operator's assistant, calendars, tasks or private records.

Assistant suggestions and saved outcomes

Task recommendations, summaries and planning suggestions can be incomplete or mistaken. Check important dates, commitments and saved changes against the relevant source. An assistant reply, processing reaction or empty calendar result alone does not prove that a change was saved or that a period is free.

The current Google Calendar integration is read-only. Requests to create, edit or delete Calendar events are outside that integration's permissions. Task capture and correction use a separate connected task system and must follow the owner's authorization boundaries.

Availability and changes

Timmy depends on the owner's computer, network, connected services and valid authorization. Continuous availability, uninterrupted authorization and error-free responses are not guaranteed. The operator may change or discontinue this personal setup. Material changes to Google data use should be reflected in the privacy policy before the expanded use begins.

Privacy and control

The Privacy policy explains Google data access, AI and messaging providers, retained records and access removal. Revoking a connection stops future authorized use subject to token validity; it does not automatically delete existing records or messages.

Third-party services

Google, the configured AI provider, Telegram, Notion and the website host have their own terms and privacy practices. Timmy is an independent personal application and is not presented as a Google product or endorsement.

Questions

Existing users should use the private assistant channel or the support contact displayed in Google's consent configuration. No public sign-up or paid support service is offered on this website.
